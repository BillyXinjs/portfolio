Industry Scenario Playbook — 21 Scenarios × 9 Functional Tracks

2026
AI enablement · knowledge asset design

Most AI rollouts stall because every team re-invents the prompt. I turned 21 delivered scenarios into a structured playbook across 9 functional tracks, each reproducing the full “prompt → deliverable” chain so a colleague can pick it up and run it.

  • 9 tracks: market insight · social listening · brand marketing · business analytics · supply chain · offline retail · HR · finance · office productivity.
  • 16 online deliverables attached, so each scenario ends in an artifact rather than a description.
  • Adopted team-wide — scenario production moved from “write a prompt from scratch” to “copy from the playbook”, roughly 80% less build time.
Note: the original playbook and its deliverables were built on a former employer's internal platform and are not publicly shareable. What is described here is the structure and method, not the source material.
